Connect page, two columns

The connect screen is one responsive page, not two products. At and above 900 px it opens out into a page: a slim header carrying the mark, the wordmark and exactly two ghost links; under it the product's statement on the left — mark, headline, the two-sentence body, the privacy promise, an ink rule and the mono facts line, the OG card's composition — and on the right the card slimmed to its task, the label, the field, the hint, the cobalt Connect and the no-code line; a mono footer across the bottom. Below 900 px it is exactly the phone card from the previous mock, mark and all, with no header bar, because mobile-first is untouched and the card already carries the mark.

The disclosure holds exactly those two sentences and opens under the line, so the attribution is one click from every screen and on none of them by default. It is the same About the phone card's footer line carries.

The cat is an interim mark — content-a.svg, the PM's pick, at 24 px in the header and 48 px in the statement, pending the founder's choice. Nothing in this layout depends on which mark wins.

Colours, type sizes and rules transcribed from web/src/styles.css by way of the previous mock; the card, its Paste ghost, its hint states and every line of its copy are unchanged. Page chrome adds three things only: the 52 px header, the statement column, and the footer line. Contrast, both schemes: ink 20.4:1 / 21:1 · muted 6.3:1 / 8.0:1 · cobalt links 6.7:1 / 7.5:1 (dark links use the #7E93FF tint, never flat cobalt on black) · Connect's white on cobalt 6.7:1 · the valid-hint check 5.4:1 / 11:1 — AA or better throughout. The breakpoint is simulated here by a class rather than a media query, because a media query answers to the browser window and not to a scaled render frame; in the stylesheet it is one @media (min-width:900px) block, and the per-frame gutter, gap, card width and headline size become a clamp.
