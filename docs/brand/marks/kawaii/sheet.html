Infercat, big-eyed marks

Revision 3 (ears bigger, rounder, edges bowed; anchored inside the head). Five marks at the proportion of the founder’s reference: a wide head, eyes nearly half its width set low, a large pupil with a highlight, small rounded ears at the corners. One colour throughout, so every mark inverts on black and sits on cobalt unchanged. Each at 160, 64, 32 and 16 px on paper, 64, 32 and 16 on black, 64 in cobalt, and in the wordmark lockup on both grounds.

PM recommendation: one face, two crops. Face is the favicon and every mark under 48 px, because its eyes survive there. Peek is the same face used large: on the connect page, the OG card and the README, where its paws over the edge tell the story and it is the reference’s pose. If you would rather have a single drawing everywhere, Face alone. Seated keeps the tail and the Swiss body if you prefer a whole cat.
