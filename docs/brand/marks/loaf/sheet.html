Infercat, the loaf

Five loafs, same head and ears (rev 3: big rounded ears anchored inside the head, eyes at the reference proportion with a pupil and a highlight). They differ in the body: which corners are rounded, how wide it is, where the head sits, and whether there is a tail. One colour throughout. Each at 160, 64, 32 and 16 px on paper, 64, 32 and 16 on black, 64 in cobalt, and in the wordmark lockup on both grounds.

PM read. Rounded top is exactly what you asked for and is the safest. Squat is the cutest, because the chin on the loaf is the pose cats actually take. Wide is the most loaf and the strongest silhouette at 16 px. I would pick Squat for the mark and use it everywhere; if you want the tail gone from the favicon, No tail is the same drawing without it.
