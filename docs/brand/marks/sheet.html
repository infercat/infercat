Infercat mark sheet

Eight revisions of the Swiss ink cat, briefed to be cuter and not ghostly. Each at 160/64/32/16 px ink on paper, 64/32/16 paper on black, and in cobalt. Same wordmark lockup at 28 px on both grounds. Ordered by the PM’s ranking.

PM pick: the sitting content cat (1). Cute without a single hollow eye, and the closed eyes are a slow blink, which is the gesture a cat uses to say it trusts you. If you would rather have no face at all, the back-turned sitter (2) is the purest Swiss form and cannot be misread. The seated kitten (3) is the cutest open-eyed option but keeps a little of the hollow-eye read you asked to lose. Whichever you choose is a one-file swap on the brand branch.
