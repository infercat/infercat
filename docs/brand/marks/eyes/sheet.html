Round eyes, six variants

One body, six eyes. Circle head, two triangle ears, one triangle body, one stroked arc tail — identical in every variant, byte for byte. Eyes are holes cut by reverse winding; a pupil is an ink subpath inside the hole. One colour throughout (currentColor).

One fix applied. At the specified base spacing the two r 2.10 ring holes left a 1.60-unit bridge — 0.8 px at 16 — and fused into a single grey bar. The pair was widened by 0.8, the same widening dots-large-low uses; radii and the eye line are as specified. Every hole now clears the head rim by ≥ 1.04 units and the body edge by ≥ 0.71. Head, ears, body and tail are byte-identical across all six; dots-medium is byte-identical to geometry-a.svg.
